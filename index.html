FAQ

Questions, answered.

Everything you need to know before getting started with RIMG.

What is RIMG? +

RIMG is a platform that helps eligible traders access cashback or rebates associated with applicable trading activity.

How do cashback and rebates work? +

Eligible trading activity may generate rebates according to the applicable broker, account and RIMG arrangements. The amount and eligibility can vary.

Do I need to change how I trade? +

No. You continue trading according to your own strategy. RIMG does not require you to change your trading approach.

How do I get verified? +

After creating your account and adding an eligible trading account, RIMG reviews the information provided as part of the verification process.

When are rewards reflected? +

Reward timing depends on the applicable trading activity, broker reporting and verification processes. Rewards are reflected when they have been confirmed and processed.

Which trading accounts are eligible? +

Eligibility depends on the supported broker, trading account and applicable RIMG arrangements. You can check eligible account options when signing up.

How do withdrawals work? +

Eligible rewards can be requested for withdrawal from your available balance, subject to verification, eligibility requirements and the applicable withdrawal terms.
